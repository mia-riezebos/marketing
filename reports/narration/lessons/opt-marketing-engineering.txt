Marketing engineering as a stack, not a personality

Korzhov lists eleven operator skills from ads agents to incrementality. Cody argues the “100x marketer” now outranks the “100x engineer” if they can wire tools. Both posts also sell software. Take the map, leave the pitch.

Takeaway: You do not need eleven systems. You need to know the map so you do not confuse a missing tracking event with a missing strategy.

Dmitry Korzhov posted that if you learn marketing engineering you will never be unemployed again, then walked through eleven steps. Several of those steps already appeared in the core course (review research, assistant citations, matching the page to the promise, tracking that does not double-count, holdouts, incrementality, and a weekly report). This optional module is the map of the rest.

Cody Schneider argues that a marketer who can wire assistants is now more leveraged than a legendary engineer, and he promotes Graphed as a place to deploy those agents. Greg Isenberg wrote a nearby opinion that “cracked” marketers are in demand. Those are opinions and product promotions. They are not assignments.

Korzhov’s thread also points at a repo. You do not need it to understand the layers.

The eleven, in one line each

- An ads agent that can work inside rules and caps, so it cannot spend without a limit.
- Programmatic SEO: many useful pages from structured data, not hundreds of thin copies of the same outline.
- AI video ads at volume, which still need a true demonstration and brand rules.
- Rebuilding competitor winners (the research half of the ads-library lesson).
- Getting cited by assistants (the AEO lesson).
- Customer research from a very large review set (next to Guillaume’s raw-material method).
- Landing pages that match the ad, so the promise survives the click.
- Conversion tracking that counts each sale once.
- Mail and messages that fire on behavior, with a holdout group.
- Incrementality: what the channel really added.
- A report that compares periods and suggests the next test.

Layers 1 to 3 are easy to abuse (uncapped spend, doorway pages, generic video). If you ever build them, keep Hunt’s ask-first list and Addy’s ownership line in place.

Cody’s extra notes about making many on-brand ads for a few hundred dollars are vendor claims about cost. They do not replace a creative point of view.

What to do

Under 2 minutes. Circle one layer or write “none yet.”

Optional, about 10 minutes. Rewrite the eleven lines and mark each against the core course. Do not install tools.

You're done when

You can see the stack and know what you already have.
