Show up when people ask an assistant

Answer engine optimization means being a source assistants can cite when someone asks a buying question. Diagnose why you are missing before you write another page.

Takeaway: If an assistant recommends someone else, the next move is to learn which source it trusted and why, not to publish a random blog post.

Answer engine optimization, often shortened to AEO, is the work of being a source that assistants can cite when someone asks a question. It overlaps with ordinary search. It is not the same thing.

jam wrote two case walkthroughs that circulated widely: one about Granola and one about Ramp. Continuations of those posts credit Discovered Labs as the vendor origin of the Granola writeup and Profound as the vendor origin of the Ramp writeup. The original vendor papers are not in this corpus, and the reported percentages were not independently audited here. What we can teach is the method jam laid out, with those credits attached.

He also offers audits in replies. That is his business. It is not your homework.

Start from buying decisions

The Granola workflow does not begin with a pile of keywords. It begins with questions people ask before they choose a tool in that category. Those same decisions then become Google queries and AI prompts, so you can see where you actually appear.

If you are missing, diagnose before you create anything:

- Is the right page missing?
- Does a page exist but fail to answer the question?
- Is there a crawl or indexing problem?
- Is the assistant getting the answer from third parties that barely mention you?

Separating “missing page” from “weak page” changes the work. If a relevant page exists, improve it. New pages appear only when there is a real gap. That is the same instinct as Andrei’s loop, pointed at assistants as well as search.

Technical issues are prioritized by whether they touch pages tied to real buyer demand. A broken important page beats a site-wide warning on a page nobody asks for.

Four kinds of sources

For each important source an assistant already trusts, the writeup uses a simple split:

- OWN. Pages you control. Improve them or create the missing one.
- CLAIM. Profiles and listings you can take ownership of.
- EARN. Editorial mentions, reviews, and community discussions you have to deserve.
- CONTEST. Outdated or wrong information that should be corrected.

Different source, different action. A missing comparison page is an own problem. An unclaimed listing is a claim problem. A trusted review site that never tested you is an earn problem. A page that says you shut down last year is a contest problem.

The writeup also says Reddit was treated as part of search, but only in communities close to the product and the buyer, using the same tracked questions. That is not permission to spam forums. If you ever join a community, follow its rules and talk like a person. This course will not ask you to post there.

Measure the two systems apart

A page can rise on Google and barely move inside ChatGPT. AI mentions can rise while ordinary search traffic stays flat. The workflow tracks both, then reruns the same questions after one specific change.

The reported Granola results include a 4,600 percent rise in monthly new users from AI search, plus large lifts in non-branded citations and mentions, and a 134 percent rise in ordinary sessions. Those are vendor-case figures as presented by jam, not numbers you should expect to copy.

A narrower version of the same idea

The Ramp walkthrough starts even smaller. They picked a category where they barely appeared (accounts payable), noted a baseline visibility figure of 3.2 percent, and studied what assistants were already citing. Comparisons and automation content kept showing up, and only a small share of Ramp’s existing citations came from that kind of page.

They then created pages for specific buying decisions, such as accounts payable software for small businesses versus large ones, and supported those pages with nearby questions about automation and AI. The reported results: more than 300 citations from the two targeted pages in a month, visibility moving from 3.2 percent to 22.2 percent, and a rise from 19th to 8th among fintech brands in that category. Again, those are presented vendor-case figures.

The portable lesson jam draws: your next opening may be a buying question your site has not answered properly yet. Ask which questions you should appear for, who is recommended today, which pages are cited, and whether you have a page that answers the full question. Then run a small experiment: choose one question, improve or create the page, rerun the prompts, and see whether citations become actual visitors and customers.

Visibility is a signal. Customers tell you whether it is working.

A weekly measurement habit, without hype

Dmitry Korzhov’s “getting cited by ChatGPT” step describes asking ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity a set of real buyer questions, logging which brands and URLs get cited, and then rewriting your most important pages to share traits of the cited ones. He names a direct answer in the first paragraph, original numbers, and a clear author. He also mentions files such as llms.txt and schema markup as ways to help machines understand a site.

You do not need his “100 questions every Monday” as a quota. Ten honest buyer questions, rerun after you change something, teach the same habit. His larger marketing-engineering list is promotional and optional later.

A concrete example

A shared shopping-list product might track prompts such as “best shared grocery list for housemates” and “how do I stop my household buying milk twice.” If an assistant cites a roundup article that never mentions you, that is an earn or contest problem, not a reason to publish a fifth blog post with the same outline as everyone else. If you already have a page that never answers “landlord deposit” or “housemates who use different phones,” that is a weak-page problem.

Hypothetical Maal example

This example is invented for teaching.

Buyer questions might include “how do I decide dinner with ADHD” and “what helps when I freeze at 17:40.” If assistants cite generic meal-planning roundups, the work is to answer the decision problem clearly on a page you own, with a real author and a first paragraph that actually decides what the reader should understand. It is not to publish “10 healthy recipes.”

What to do

Under 2 minutes. Write five questions a careful buyer would ask.

Optional, about 15 minutes. Label each question own, claim, earn, contest, or “diagnose first.” Do not buy an AEO tool and do not post in communities for the exercise.

You're done when

You can explain the four source types, and you know why search rankings and AI mentions are not the same score.
