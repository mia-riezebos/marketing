Default category people would put me in today

Five steps (Dunford)

- What I thought I was building (and what I actually deliver now)
- Uniqueness (what I have that others do not, without judging yet)
- Value of that uniqueness (what it does for someone)
- Who cares a lot (how I would recognize them this month)
- Market frame that makes the strength obvious

Six questions (Kunal)

- What do we do? What problem do we solve?
- In which category do we compete?
- Why is our approach better?
- Unique attributes and key benefits
- Who would be most interested, and how would we find them?
- If not us, what would they choose?
