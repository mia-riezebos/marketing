# Research bots for short video, not autoposters

About 7 minutes to read. Optional exercise about 8 minutes. These are estimates.

jaffa published a guide to ten Grok Bots that watch a live database of more than 21 million viral videos and help run marketing across short-video platforms. A later post shared the same guide. The workflow asks you to connect a named database (Virlo) with an API key. That signup is not part of this course.

What transfers without the vendor stack is the job split:

- Scan a niche for unusual openings and formats
- Send a short list of ideas, each tied to a real example
- Track what competitors posted, what took off, and which ads they keep paying for
- Turn a voice note into a few scripts you could film
- Have one “head of social” read the rest and produce a weekly plan

Build them once, jaffa writes, and they keep going in the background. Then you pick what to film.

That last sentence is the charter lesson again. Research can run on a schedule after clean trials. Publishing should stay a draft you send yourself. A database of other people’s videos is also not yours to copy shot-for-shot; use it to notice formats, then make your own proof.

## What to do

**Under 2 minutes.** Write one Monday research question.

**Optional, about 8 minutes.** Mark five research jobs as unattended or draft-only.

## You're done when

You can separate research automation from autoposting.
