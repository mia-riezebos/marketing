# Frequency notes, without a streak

About 6 minutes to read. Optional exercise about 5 minutes. These are estimates.

Nicholas Dulait wrote that he crossed 5,000 followers on X in five months and listed what he did. Some items are quality habits: pick one topic, lead with proof, write things people save, keep links out of the post, quote your own deeper pieces, double down on what worked. Some items are volume: publish every day, post two or three times a day, never stop.

The follower count and the volume are his report. This course will not turn them into a streak or a daily quota. If a daily rhythm would make you quit, it is the wrong rhythm.

Tuan Le posted an agenda for a video called “The Science Behind Breaking the Algorithm.” The timestamps say he talks about copying what already works, years wasted trying to be original, formats the system already recognizes, and the difference between no views, no saves, and no shares. The video itself was not watched in this corpus, so we stop at that agenda. Observation of working formats is compatible with Ryan’s hook types. It is not permission to copy someone else’s proof.

If you want a rhythm, pick one you can keep. Ship when you have one idea and a takeaway. That is already the core writing lesson.

## What to do

**Under 2 minutes.** Write an honest rhythm, including a slow one.

**Optional, about 5 minutes.** Copy only Nicholas’s non-volume items.

## You're done when

You have a rhythm and no streak.
