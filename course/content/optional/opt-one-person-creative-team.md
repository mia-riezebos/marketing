# The one-person creative team path

About 8 minutes to read. Optional exercise about 8 minutes. These are estimates.

Dan Koe’s 2027 letter argues that the most lucrative skill has always been capturing attention. Software, books, and career skills do not get paid if nobody knows they exist. Other people, he writes, will also pay you to capture attention for them.

Mogul later shared a condensed version of that argument. Dan authored it.

He describes two versions of a one-person creative team: run those skills on your own brand, or become the creative team for founders who do not have time. He mentions income in the range of $10 to $75 thousand a month for one to three clients if you are good and you practice, and he cites large creator-economy totals. Those figures are his claims and citations, not a beginner target, and not a reason to quit the product path in this course.

Inside the work he splits three jobs people often mash together:

- Brand strategy: positioning and perception. This is the person who says you should not chase every trend.
- Content strategy: what is working in the topic, which formats to try, what to make next.
- Drafting and publishing: the actual posts, scripts, and letters.

Most people, he writes, skip the first, half-do the second, and ask an assistant to pump out the third. That is the competition, and it is not hard to stand out by doing the first job.

He also describes interviewing a client to build their “intellectual signature,” and he promotes Eden, his own tool, plus a paid writing workshop. Those are his products. They are not assignments.

If this path interests you, the core course already taught the pieces you would sell: interviews, positioning, one idea per post, and a vehicle you can keep. If it does not interest you, skip the rest. You do not need an agency to market something you are building.

## What to do

**Under 2 minutes.** Choose “my product,” “other people’s brands,” or “not now.”

**Optional, about 8 minutes.** Write one sentence each for the three jobs. Do not start an agency.

## You're done when

You can tell this career path apart from the core course, and you have labeled the income range as his claim.
