## Five steps (Dunford)

1. What I thought I was building (and what I actually deliver now)

2. Uniqueness (what I have that others do not, without judging yet)

3. Value of that uniqueness (what it does for someone)

4. Who cares a lot (how I would recognize them this month)

5. Market frame that makes the strength obvious

## Six questions (Kunal)

1. What do we do? What problem do we solve?
2. In which category do we compete?
3. Why is our approach better?
4. Unique attributes and key benefits
5. Who would be most interested, and how would we find them?
6. If not us, what would they choose?
